Privacy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This site runs pétanque competitions: registration, draws, pistes, live scores and standings. It is a volunteer-run tool, not a commercial service. This page says plainly what it stores and why.

No cookies, no tracking

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers, so there is no cookie banner. Everything it keeps on your device is there only to make the site work:

WhatWhy
An anonymous sign-in ID (a random code with no name attached)So the device that creates a competition is recognised as its organiser, and so organiser or team links keep working on that device. It is created by Firebase Authentication and kept in your browser's storage.
Your settings: language, the competition and tab you last opened, the team you marked as "This is me", and whether you dismissed the "Add to home screen" bannerSo the site opens the way you left it.
An offline copy of the competitions you have openedSo the site opens instantly and keeps working with no signal; scores entered offline are sent when the signal returns.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this. If you do that on an organiser's device, that device loses its organiser access to its competitions (the organiser link restores it).

What is stored on the server

Competition data lives in Google Firebase (Firestore), in Google's London (europe-west2) region. It holds:

  • What organisers type in: competition name, venue and date, team and player names, pistes, draws and scores.
  • The anonymous ID of the device that created each competition, and a rough device label such as "iPhone · Safari", so the site admin can tell competitions apart. No name, email or precise location is attached.
  • For the site admin only: the Google account email used to sign in to the admin page.

Google processes this data on the site's behalf under its Firebase privacy terms. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Who can see competition data

Competitions are shared by link. Anyone with a competition's link can see its draw, results and standings, including the names entered for teams and players. Organisers decide how widely they share a link. Example competitions used to show how the site works are visible to everyone.

During open testing, anyone who opens a competition can also run it. Organisers can archive a finished competition, after which only the site admin can see it.

Players' names

Organisers enter players' names so that the draw and results make sense on the day. Usually that is a first name and surname, or a team name; organisers are asked not to enter anything more than that. If your name appears in a competition and you would like it changed or removed, ask the organiser who ran the event, or contact us below.

How long data is kept

Competition data stays until the organiser deletes it or the site admin removes it. Old competitions may be removed by the site admin from time to time. The anonymous sign-in ID and offline copy stay on your device until you clear your browser's site data.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to see the personal data held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its use. As the site holds very little that identifies anyone, most requests simply mean removing a name from a competition. Email the site owner and it will be dealt with promptly. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Fonts and other third parties

The fonts, icons and code are served from this site itself, so visiting it sends nothing to a font or script provider. The only third party involved is Google Firebase, described above. Signing in with Google (admin only) is subject to Google's privacy policy.
